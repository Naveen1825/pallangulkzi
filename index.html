How to Play Pallanguzhi

      
    

    

      Each of the 14 pits starts with 5 icosahedra. On your turn, click any non-empty pit on your side (Player 1 owns the near row; CPU / Player 2 owns the far row).
    

    

      The hand picks up all seeds and drops them counter-clockwise one per pit. If the pit after your last drop has seeds, the hand scoops and continues (relay). If that next pit is empty, you capture all seeds from the pit after it!
